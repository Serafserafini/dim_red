# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for `src/dim_red/supcon/` (moved out of the repo-root `CLAUDE.md` since it's specific to this directory).

`supcon/` is an encoder-only counterpart to `vae/`/`autoencoder/` — no decoder, no reconstruction loss, no KL/`beta` at all. Structured as a **body** (`SupConEncoder`, producing a representation `r`) plus, at any given moment, exactly **one attachable tail** (`tails.py`), loosely following Khosla et al. 2020's SupCon recipe generalized to three tail kinds: a `ProjectionTail` attached during the body's own (only) training stage, and a `ClassificationTail`/`VisualizationTail` attachable afterwards, one at a time, once the body is frozen.

- `model.py`: `EncoderModule` (Flax `nn.Module`, deliberately duplicated from `autoencoder.model.EncoderModule` rather than imported, so this package stays independent of `vae`/`autoencoder`) plus `SupConEncoder`, the high-level wrapper (`params`, `encode`/`encode_with_params`) — the body. Unmodified by the tail architecture below: still no `Decoder`, no classifier heads, no `codec.py` baked into it — tails are always separate Flax modules with their own independently-initialized `params` pytrees, never nested inside the body's.
- `tails.py`: `ProjectionTail`/`VisualizationTail` (small MLPs, `r -> R^d`, sharing a private `_MLPTail` Flax module — structurally and loss-identical, differing only in output width and which stage attaches them) and `ClassificationTail` (`ClassifierHead`/`apply_family_mask`, verbatim duplicates of `vae.model`'s, plus a `ClassificationTailModule` mirroring `VAEModule`'s head setup minus the encoder/decoder it also owns). Every tail's `params` is a wholly separate pytree from the body's `SupConEncoder.params` — never a sub-tree reached by traversing it — which is what lets phase 1 (below) compose them as two independently-keyed dict branches (`{"body": ..., "tail": ...}`) and what lets phase 2 simply omit the body's pytree from that dict entirely to "freeze" it, with no `jax.lax.stop_gradient`/`optax.multi_transform`/masked-optimizer machinery needed anywhere (there is no other freezing precedent in this codebase).
- `training.py` (**phase 1**): `TrainConfig` (`epochs`/`batch_size`/`learning_rate` — unused, VeLO again — /`tau`/`seed`/`device`; deliberately *without* `lambda_family`/`lambda_spacegroup`/`lambda_norm`, which live in `pipeline.config.SupConConfig` instead and are passed to `train_supcon` as explicit arguments — none of the three are training-loop mechanics like `tau`, they're loss-term weights). `train_supcon(model, projection_tail, train_db, val_db, config, ...)` jointly trains the body and a `ProjectionTail` — `params` is `{"body": model.params, "tail": projection_tail.params}`, one `jax.value_and_grad` differentiates both at once, and both are written back (`model.params`/`projection_tail.params`) at the end. `supcon_loss(z, labels, tau)` implements Supervised Contrastive (Khosla et al. 2020) with a *negative squared-Euclidean-distance* similarity (`-||z_i - z_j||^2 / tau`, not the more common cosine-similarity formulation — no internal L2-normalization of `z`), and for each anchor sums over same-label positives in the batch (self excluded) normalized by a log-sum-exp over every other batch row. An anchor with no positive in its batch is excluded from that term's mean (not zeroed-and-diluted) — implemented by dividing by the count of anchors that *did* have a positive, not the batch size. A large finite negative sentinel (not `-jnp.inf`) masks self-comparisons before `log_softmax`, since `0 * -inf` is NaN in the forward pass, not just the gradient — a real pitfall if you touch this function. Crucially, `supcon_loss`/`norm_penalty` are computed on **the projection tail's output `z = Proj(r)`, not the body's own representation `r`** (Khosla et al. 2020's actual recipe) — pure functions over any latent batch, so this is just a matter of which tensor `_make_train_step`/`_make_eval_step` feed them, and `lambda_norm`'s regularization target moves with it. `norm_penalty(z)` (`mean(sum(z**2, axis=-1))`) is available as an optional regularizer, weighted by `lambda_norm` (`0.0` — disabled — by default, unlike `lambda_family`/`lambda_spacegroup` not gated by `mode` since it doesn't depend on labels at all) and added to `total` in both `_make_train_step`/`_make_eval_step` identically, so `val_loss` reflects the same objective actually optimized; `train_norm_penalty`/`val_norm_penalty` are always in `history`, regardless of `lambda_norm`, for diagnosing unbounded embedding growth even when the penalty itself is off. `train_supcon` mirrors `train_vae`/`train_autoencoder`'s `has_family`/`has_spacegroup` compile-time-branch pattern and VeLO optimizer setup, but deliberately does **not** reuse their padding+`vmap`-over-epoch validation trick: SupCon is pairwise *within* a batch, so a zero-padded dummy row would become a spurious positive/negative for real rows (unlike MSE/cross-entropy, computed independently per row). Both train and validation instead use a plain unpadded batch loop (one jitted call per batch), epoch metrics combined as a batch-size-weighted mean.
- `tail_training.py` (**phase 2**): `TailTrainConfig` (training-loop mechanics only — mirrors `training.TrainConfig`'s shape) plus `train_classification_tail`/`train_visualization_tail`, each training exactly one tail against a **frozen body's already-computed representations**, passed in directly as plain `r_train`/`r_val` arrays (not a `VAEDatabase` — these are fixed representations to train a small head on, not raw features to standardize/encode, and there's no body forward pass at all in either loop, hence no `SupConEncoder` argument here). `train_classification_tail` mirrors `autoencoder.training`'s aux-head cross-entropy/`apply_family_mask` shape; `train_visualization_tail` reuses `supcon_loss`/`norm_penalty` directly from `training.py` (genuinely shared pure math, not duplicated) computed on the visualization tail's own output, and supports the same `"random"`/`"balanced"` batching choice as phase 1 (via `sampling.iter_balanced_batches`). `_iter_batches`/`_weighted_mean` are duplicated here (matching the existing `_iter_batches` duplication precedent between `autoencoder.training`/`supcon.training`). See `dim_red.pipeline.tail_training` (`src/dim_red/pipeline/CLAUDE.md`) for the end-to-end entry point that supplies `r_train`/`r_val` from a completed run's `embeddings.npz`.
- `sampling.py`: an alternative to plain random shuffling for *training* batches only (validation batches always stay random, regardless), selected via `BatchingConfig.strategy == "balanced"` (`pipeline.config`) — used by both phase 1's `train_supcon` and phase 2's `train_visualization_tail`. `iter_balanced_batches`/`balanced_batch_indices` build each batch as `P` families x `K` examples per family, always further stratified across spacegroups within each family, `K // S_eff` examples each — the remainder `K % S_eff` going to the first few chosen spacegroups when it doesn't divide evenly. `S` (`BalancedBatchingParams.S`) picks how many spacegroups per family: `None` (default) uses *every* spacegroup present for that family (`S_eff = n_spacegroups_present`), an explicit integer samples only that many (clamped down, with a `logger.warning`, if it exceeds what's present) — so the SupCon loss reliably has positives available for every anchor even on less-balanced datasets, and by default batches are as evenly spread across a family's spacegroups as `K` allows. `P` is clamped the same way. Because spacegroup stratification is always applied now, `spacegroup_ids` is a required argument to both functions (not optional) — callers always build spacegroup ids whenever `batching.strategy == "balanced"`, regardless of the active loss `mode`/`S`. Row sampling is without replacement when the pool is large enough, with replacement only when necessary (a small family/spacegroup). Uses `logging.getLogger("dim_red.pipeline")` — the same logger name every other pipeline module uses, not a `dim_red.supcon`-prefixed one — purely so these warnings land in a run's `run.log` (a plain `logging` name, not an import, so this doesn't create a dependency on `dim_red.pipeline`).
- Phase 1 reuses `vae.database.VAEDatabase` directly, same as `autoencoder` (phase 2 doesn't — see `tail_training.py` above).
- `pipeline.single_run.run_single` integrates `model_kind == "supcon"` as a third branch alongside `vae`/`autoencoder` (model + `ProjectionTail` construction, `train_config`, the training call, and `_log_epoch`'s per-history-key logging), all additive — the `vae`/`autoencoder` branches are untouched. It reads its encoder architecture from the same `vae:` block (`decoder_hidden_dim`/`mirror` ignored, like `autoencoder` ignores `beta`) but its own `supcon:` block (YAML: `mode` — `"family_only"`/`"spacegroup_only"`/`"family_and_spacegroup"`, mirroring `aux_heads.mode`'s shape but independent per term, since there's no family→spacegroup masking to require family whenever spacegroup is active — plus `lambda_family`/`lambda_spacegroup`/`tau`/`lambda_norm`/`projection_dim` (Khosla et al. 2020 default `128`, the projection tail's output width, separate from `vae.latent_dim`)/`projection_hidden_dim` (`None` -> one hidden layer matching `vae.latent_dim`)) instead of `aux_heads:`, and its batch-sampling strategy from `batching:` (`pipeline.config.BatchingConfig` — `strategy: random` (default) or `balanced`, plus `balanced_params: {P, K, S}`; ignored for `vae`/`autoencoder`, which always shuffle). The trained `ProjectionTail`'s params are saved to a 5th run artifact, `projection_params.msgpack` (discarded after phase 1 in the original paper; kept here for reproducibility, though nothing downstream ever reloads it — `inference.load_trained_run`'s required-file set stays at 4 entries). Since the body itself still has no classifier heads, `run_single`'s family/spacegroup-probability inference block is still skipped entirely for this `model_kind`; `embeddings.npz`'s `embeddings` field is still the body's raw `r` (unchanged by any of this — the projection tail's output never gets saved there), and the file keeps only the base fields (`embeddings`/`features`/`labels`/`material_ids`/`spacegroups`/`split`), no `*_probs`/`*_classes`. Note `train_supcon`'s `train_family_ids`/`train_spacegroup_ids` (which family/spacegroup terms the *loss* uses) are deliberately independent of its `batching_family_ids`/`batching_spacegroup_ids` (which labels *batches* are grouped by) — `run_single` builds family/spacegroup vocab ids whenever either is needed, so e.g. `supcon.mode: spacegroup_only` + `batching.strategy: balanced` still groups batches by family even though the family loss term itself stays inactive. See `configs/single_run_supcon.example.yaml`/`configs/sweep_supcon.example.yaml`.

See the repo-root `CLAUDE.md` for the Optimizer-selection and Early-stopping conventions, which apply here too (duplicated identically across `vae.training`/`autoencoder.training`/`supcon.training`), and `src/dim_red/pipeline/CLAUDE.md` for phase 2's CLI entry point (`pipeline.tail_training`) and the `RunConfig.tails` auto-invoke mechanism.
