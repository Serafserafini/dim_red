Why a classical symmetry finder is not enough

Noisy structures

Monte Carlo at finite T: displaced atoms, vacancies, some far from any local minimum of the PES

Classical tools fail

Symmetry finders like spglib need every atom within a small tolerance of its ideal site

Local optimisation is not enough

Costly (only a subset), and it stops at the nearest minimum — no guarantee it is the real phase
